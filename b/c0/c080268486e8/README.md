1. _Pr._ Thy wish to know is good, and happy is he
2. _Pr._ Wide-horned, large-eyed, broad-fronted, and the feet
3. Which said, when he agreed, she spake no more,
4. Nor till again they came upon the road,
5. For she too hearing gan her heart exalt,
6. Nor now was Aphrodite kinder grown:
7. ‘Let now that I have saved thee twice outweigh
8. In all things beautiful, I cannot see
