1. But when he had her there, sharp root of ill
2. Now calleth he assembly of the chiefs,
3. High over Troy the windy citadel,
4. There in her cage roamed Helen light and fierce,
5. The Maid stood still
6. Now rides he out, a dubbed knight,
7. We read that lovers' tale; each lookt at each;
