## 章节目录（前 10 段）

1. O throbbing heart, be still!
2. For oh, I have loved thee well,
3. "'No good?'"--said a willow tree, bending low
4. THE ONE REFUGE.
5. Old Aleck, the weaver, sat in the nook
6. _Thou canst not?_--what! hast thou not entered
7. Come home!--the vine that climbs our cottage eaves,
8. Honor to Labor!--it diveth deep
9. But we who know thee, proudly point the hand
10. Is light a blessing?--He's the soul's clear LIGHT--
