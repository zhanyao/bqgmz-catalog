1. The child stared at me. "To the Countess Scarabelli."
2. My young Englishman began to smile. "Of whom are you speaking?"
3. Instantly, instinctively, he raised his hand to my arm. "Truly?"
4. He glanced at me again. "On the contrary, I like to hear."
5. He hesitated a moment. "One can't express that."
6. For a moment he said nothing. "A month."
7. I was silent a moment. "I should like to see that."
8. I shook my head. "For me it was no mistake."
9. She dropped her head a little to one side. "For an Englishman--no!"
