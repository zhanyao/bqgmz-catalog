## 章节目录（前 10 段）

1. There is a rushing in the woods,
2. Yea, ev’n the mad, marauding Wind,
3. If I should find thee sitting
4. Hush! she is dead. Tread gently as the light
5. THE MOONMEN
6. See, where the roses, at the wood’s dark edge,
7. High on the world did our fathers of old,
8. Forth, forth, my heart, and let us find our dreams,
9. And then she rose, and to the casement hastened,
10. Something the sunset points at from afar,
