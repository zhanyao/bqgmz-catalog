1. OF me and of my theme think what thou wilt:
2. Nor had saffron and sapphire and red
3. Water, first of singers, o’er rocky mount and mead,

5. XXII
6. Seen of Attila, desired,
7. To prove the Gods benignant to his rule,
8. But noon beheld a larger heaven;
9. Budding, the service-tree, white
