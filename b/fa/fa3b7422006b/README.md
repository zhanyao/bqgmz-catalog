## 章节目录（前 7 段）

1. "Oh! fire away, ye villains, and earn King George's shillin'
2. On the shore, dimly seen through the mists of the deep,
3. He--marking how her high increase
4. Forty flags with their silver stars,
5. But as we ranged ahead,
6. He makes no fuss about the job,
7. Our boys have seen the Old World as none have seen before.
