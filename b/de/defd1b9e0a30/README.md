## 章节目录（前 10 段）

1. Farewell to them: yet pause ere thou unmoor
2. "The race is like a child, as yet
3. Never! 'Tis certain that no hope is--none!
4. I'm glad that echo was not heard
5. "Cusha! Cusha! Cusha!" calling,
6. We reached the place by night,
7. So as it chanced we lighted on a path
8. She had but sixteen years, and as for me,
9. The low depending curtains, gently swayed,
10. _Here is the ending of the second dream._
