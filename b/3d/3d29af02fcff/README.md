## 章节目录（前 9 段）

1. Mother, dear mother, the years have been long
2. And like those waters rushing
3. I walk down the Valley of Silence—
4. Abou Ben Adhem (may his tribe increase!)
5. ’TIS THE LAST ROSE OF SUMMER.
6. Is it, O man, with such discordant noises,
7. If I were you, when ladies at the play, sir,
8. Where shall the lover rest,
9. Sally in Our Alley _Henry Carey_
