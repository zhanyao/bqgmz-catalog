## 章节目录（前 10 段）

1. Then through the red wrath of his eyne
2. Sweet thoughts fly swiftlier than the drifting snow,
3. With a grace of prayers sung loud and late
4. Ye who have come o'er the sea
5. No sooner was the word gone forth
6. "Yea," quoth she, "There doth the mistress dwell.
7. Thou art a dear thing to my life,
8. Said I not when thou knew'st it, all courage should fail the
9. Then soon was it midnight, and moonset, as we wended
10. A song in my mouth, then? my heart full of gladness?
