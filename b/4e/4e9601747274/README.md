1. She, standing in the yellow morning sun,
2. Till she that threw the golden apple down
3. Then wearily she went upon her way,
4. Then, pale and full of trouble, Psyche went
5. But as about the hall he passed
6. Then springing up he took his spear and bow
7. And so, betwixt seed-time and harvesting,
8. Her eyes were shut, but she seemed not to sleep,
