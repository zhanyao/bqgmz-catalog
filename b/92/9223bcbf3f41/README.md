## 章节目录（前 10 篇）

1. O! should I ever dare profane
2. Page
3. 'Glad Envy hisses, Ridicule and Scorn
4. In music sweetly stole away;--
5. 'Here lies a bird, once famed to be
6. And that the golden blush of day.'
7. And with his song her coming hail'd.'
8. And minister his daily fare.'
9. 'Then would he peck his savoury treat,--
10. Then silent sit--now pour his throat.'
