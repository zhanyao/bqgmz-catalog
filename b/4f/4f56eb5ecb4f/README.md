## 章节目录（前 7 段）

1. Here are the needs of manhood satisfied!
2. At evening I went back,
3. More than bare mountains ’neath a naked sky,
4. Lord Christ, if Thou art with us and these eyes
5. Fatigued of heart, and owning how the world
6. Here some time flowed my springs and sent a cry
7. Song that is pent in me,
