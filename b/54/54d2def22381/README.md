## 章节目录（前 8 段）

1. The thought went by. He had heard the Bosun tell
2. A woman walked along the hall below,
3. And as we watched, there came a rush of feet
4. I cannot tell their wonder nor make known
5. I went into the house, and shut the door
6. He rose up in his place and rent his dress.
7. Sang for the setting free of men
8. The Tartar horsemen
